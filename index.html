About Me

Get to know the developer behind the code

My name is Zander A Borrelli. I was born in Ogdensburg, NY on April 27 th , 2009, and grew up in the small town of Canton, NY. My parents divorced when I was very young, so I live with my Mother Tara, My stepfather Thomas, and my 4 siblings. I go to school at Canton Central School and attend Seaway BOCES Tech. I’ve grown up with technology and my first experience with technology was with a Nintendo 64 when I was very young. I remember being shocked with awe when I saw it in action and learned how it worked. Throughout my life, there have been numerous advances in technology that have been good and bad.

What inspired me to take a computer science course at Seaway Tech was seeing the damage that can come from technology. Breaches in databases, robbery of personal information, etc. Alongside the various uses of this advancing technology in the real world. I’ve attended Canton Central since I was around 4-5 years old. I was always one of the brighter and smarter kids in my class and still am. School is nice, but it has its effects on people and led to the most impactful moment of my life. My best friend committed suicide due to cyberbullying and after finding out his motivation, it filled me with motivation to pursue a career in technology so I can help keep others safe and help others in the coming Digital Age.

I will graduate from Canton Central School and Seaway BOCES Tech in 2027, and plan on attending SUNY Canton College and majoring in Information Tech. I want to use the skills I will learn to help keep businesses safe and knowledgeable,and to try and keep others safe in the Digital Age. In ten years I see myself affiliated with some company keeping them updated in the advancing age.
